About the numbers

Formulas

Given annual income in home currency (incomeLocal):

  • your_ppp_income = incomeLocal / home.ppp
  • equiv (PPP) = your_ppp_income × dest.ppp — destination currency to live the same
  • fxLocal = incomeLocal × (dest.fx / home.fx) — wire / market convert
  • costPct = (dest.pli_us / home.pli_us − 1) × 100
  • Price level display = round(dest.pli_us × 100) with US = 100

Country data

World Bank WDI: private-consumption PPP (PA.NUS.PRVT.PP) and official FX (PA.NUS.FCRF). Price level index pli_us = ppp / fx relative to the United States. Countries with unreliable PLI (below 0.05 or non-finite) are skipped or warned — never Infinity/NaN.

Commodities

Sample prices from The Economist Big Mac Index (open GitHub CSV) and Numbeo country averages. Gaps are left null. Refresh with scripts/fetch_commodities.py.

Illustrative tax estimates

Optional national/federal personal income tax models (progressive, flat, or none) live in data/taxes.json, primarily from PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries. In Gross mode, a supported home country converts entered gross → estimated net before PPP. Destination gross-up estimates the gross needed abroad so estimated net ≈ the PPP-equivalent take-home. Deduction assumption (none / standard / typical extra / custom %) applies to both sides. Where brackets already include a 0% allowance band, standard mode does not subtract that allowance again. Employee social contributions are included only when listed. Many local/state taxes, credits, rebates, and filing-status rules are excluded. Not tax advice — illustrative for PPP context only. Unsupported countries show a warning and never invent brackets.

FAQ

What does PPP mean here?

Private-consumption purchasing-power parity from the World Bank (PA.NUS.PRVT.PP). It converts your home income into an international unit, then into destination currency at destination prices — the amount that roughly buys the same basket.

PPP equivalent vs FX / wire amount?

FX is a bank-style convert at the official rate. PPP adjusts for how expensive the destination is. Cheaper destinations show a lower PPP equivalent than the wire; expensive ones show higher.

Where do sources come from?

Country PPP/FX: World Bank WDI. Commodities: Economist Big Mac Index + Numbeo (illustrative). Tax estimates: illustrative national/federal PIT from PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries (data/taxes.json) — not tax advice.

What this is not

Not personalized tax advice and not a full tax engine — only illustrative national/federal PIT for PPP context. Not a median / local-class lab. For × median and relative class, see KingIndex.
